# Insurance Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

Developed an interactive Tableau dashboard to analyze insurance policies, customers, claims, and payment data. The project provides key business insights through KPI cards, interactive visualizations, filters, and calculated fields.

## Objectives

- Analyze insurance policy and customer trends.
- Monitor policy status and active policy percentage.
- Evaluate premium and coverage performance.
- Analyze claims and payment-related information.
- Identify trends across age groups, gender, and policy types.

## Tools & Technologies

- Tableau
- Excel
- Calculated Fields
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Dashboards

## Key KPIs

- Total Customers: 5,000
- Total Policies: 5,000
- Total Claim Amount: ₹251.38M
- Average Coverage Amount: ₹254.56K
- Average Premium Amount: ₹1.05K
- Active Policy Percentage: 33.64%

## Key Insights

- The 60–75 age group has the highest policy participation with 1,164 policies.
- Health insurance has the highest policy count with 1,316 policies.
- Policy participation is relatively balanced across genders.
- Average premium growth rate is -1.91%, indicating fluctuations over time.
- Premium growth was highest at 4.79% in 2022 and lowest at -9.74% in 2015.
- Policy endings peaked in 2025 with 526 policies.

## Dashboard Features

- Interactive KPI cards
- Policy status analysis
- Policy type analysis
- Age-group and gender analysis
